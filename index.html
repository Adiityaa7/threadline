Everything in its place.
Every connection, clear.

Threadline for Windows brings Excel workbooks, PowerPoint decks, files, and web links into a visual workspace on your computer.

From two links to one clear picture.

  1. In OneDrive or SharePoint, use Share → Copy link.
  2. In Threadline, click Add document → Add a web link.
  3. Enter a title, owner, and description. Choose Spreadsheet or Presentation, paste the web link, and save.
  4. Add your next document. In its details, under Connections, choose the related item, write a label such as “uses figures from”, and click +.
  5. Open Connection map. Drag cards to arrange them, or connect their dots to create more relationships.
  6. Click Open in browser on a map card, or Open link in its details. Your default browser opens the saved address.

Learn inside the app

Choose Help & tutorial → Start guided tutorial for a walkthrough using your own links. Choose Add document → Try Office example for a sample map with a real demo workbook and slide deck. The example OneDrive addresses are placeholders; replace them with your own sharing links.

What works offline?

Your library, owners, descriptions, search, imported files, and connections work locally. Saved web links require internet and the destination's existing permissions when opened. Threadline has no accounts, automatic cloud sync, or document server.

Keep a backup

Use Workspace settings → Export backup to save a .threadline copy on your computer. Password protection is optional. Restore verifies the backup and replaces your current workspace; keep a separate copy before restoring. The backup limit is 256 MB and the import limit is 100 MB per file.
