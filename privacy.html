Threadline for Windows — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 September 2026
Developer: Aditya Patil
Contact: adityapatil206999@gmail.com

Your local workspace Threadline is a local Windows document organizer. It has no user accounts, cloud document service, automatic synchronization, advertising, or app analytics service. The developer does not receive your documents or workspace metadata through the app.

The app stores the files you choose to import, preview images, document titles, descriptions, owner labels, tags, dates, collections, connections, and local activity history on your Windows device. Owner names are labels you enter; adding an owner does not contact that person or give them access.

The standalone Windows build keeps its profile under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Threadline\Workspace. Windows may redirect application data for a packaged Microsoft Store installation. Use the app's backup and restore controls to move a workspace. Threadline does not add encryption to the working database; your Windows account and device protections control local access.

Links and external services Saving a OneDrive, SharePoint, or other HTTP(S) link stores its address and the details you enter. Threadline does not download the document behind that link, fetch a preview from the website, or sign you into that service. Opening a link launches your default browser and contacts the destination. That website may require internet access, authentication, and permission to access the document. Its privacy policy applies.

Files and backups Imported originals remain unchanged. A preview image may be created locally. You choose when and where to export an original or a .threadline workspace backup. New workspace backups do not compress documents. Optional password protection encrypts the exported backup with AES-GCM; an unprotected export is not encrypted. The password is not sent to the developer and cannot be recovered by us.

Windows, Microsoft Edge WebView2, your browser, enterprise administration, antivirus tools, and backup software may have separate update, diagnostic, or data-handling behavior under their own settings and policies. Threadline's local design does not override these services.

Retention and deletion Items moved to Trash remain recoverable until you permanently delete them. Permanent deletion removes the app's records and any saved file content no longer referenced by another record. This is not a guarantee of forensic disk erasure.

The standalone uninstaller preserves the workspace so that reinstalling does not remove your work. To remove that workspace after exporting anything you want to keep, close Threadline and remove its profile folder shown above. Packaged installations may be reset or uninstalled through Windows Settings; first back up your work. Delete separately exported files and backups yourself. There is no server-side copy we can restore.

Support If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send confidential documents unless necessary and agreed. Your email provider and our email provider handle that correspondence under their own policies. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any retention obligations.

Changes We will update this page when Threadline's data handling changes. A version that introduces online data collection or synchronization would need a corresponding policy update and appropriate user choices.
